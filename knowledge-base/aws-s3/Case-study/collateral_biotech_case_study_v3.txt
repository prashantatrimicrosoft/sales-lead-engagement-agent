Title: Safeguarding Lab Operations with Reliable Backup Power
Doc_Type: Case Study
Industry_Tag: Biotechnology, Life Sciences, Healthcare

Summary:
A growth-stage biotechnology company's lab operations depend on continuous power for sample storage, incubation equipment, and sensitive instrumentation. A single extended outage risked not just downtime, but the loss of irreplaceable samples and months of research work - a risk the company's aging backup power setup wasn't adequately covering as the lab expanded.

The company installed a dedicated standby generator sized for its lab and cold-storage loads, along with an SLA that includes quarterly load testing and priority emergency response. When a regional outage hit shortly after installation, the generator carried the full lab load without interruption, protecting an active sample set worth months of research investment.

For biotech and life sciences organizations, where funding rounds often accelerate lab build-outs faster than facilities planning can keep up, right-sized backup power paired with an active maintenance contract is frequently the difference between a routine outage and a costly research setback.

Why it matters: in life sciences, protecting the samples in the freezer is often more valuable than protecting the equipment around them.
