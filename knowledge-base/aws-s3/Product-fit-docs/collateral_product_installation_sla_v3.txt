Title: Installation and SLA Maintenance Program - Product Overview
Doc_Type: Product One-Pager
Industry_Tag: All Industries, Manufacturing, Electronics, Biotechnology, Hospitality, Logistics, Energy

Summary:
Generator installation and ongoing maintenance service, covering the full lifecycle from initial setup through continuous reliability monitoring, so backup power systems perform when actually needed rather than only during testing.

Core offering:
- Full installation service, including electrical integration, automatic transfer switch setup, and initial load testing.
- SLA maintenance tiers covering scheduled inspections, quarterly or semi-annual load testing, and priority emergency response commitments.
- 24-hour emergency dispatch for active outages under premium SLA tiers.
- Proactive component monitoring designed to catch early signs of battery, fuel system, or transfer switch issues before they cause a failure during an actual outage.

Facilities under an active SLA see meaningfully lower unplanned-failure rates than those relying on generators without a maintenance contract, since most generator failures stem from components that degrade silently between rare activation events.

Best fit: any organization that has already invested in backup power and wants assurance the system will perform during an actual event, not just during scheduled testing.
